The Studio Difference

The Art of Digital Sculpting.

Every project begins as a highly detailed digital clay model sculpted in ZBrush, Maya, or Blender.

Unlike standard workshops that rely on rigid pre-made templates, our digital artists sculpt organic depth, micro-textures, and fine artistic nuances on screen first. Once visually perfect and approved by you, we translate the digital model into precise machine code for our CNC routers and 3D printers, ensuring your original vision comes to life with zero creative loss.

Studio Methodology

How It Works

From an initial inspiration photo to physical delivery, our transparent 4-step workflow brings any creative concept to life.

01

The Vision

Provide a Pinterest photo, rough sketch, or idea. We review your concept, help choose the right material, and determine sizing.

02

Digital Sculpting

We model it in ZBrush/Maya and you review the 3D render. You get to approve every curve, expression, and detail before anything is made.

03

Precision Manufacturing

CNC routing or 3D printing begins on our studio floor, translating digital coordinates into high-precision cuts and fine resin/fibre layers.

04

Final Production

Delivered ready to install. Post-processed, sanded, primed, or custom finished according to your aesthetic requirements.
