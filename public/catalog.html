Production Capabilities & Catalog

Engineered fabrication solutions for architects, interior designers, and bespoke creators. Structured across three master production disciplines machined directly in our Guwahati atelier.

Category 01 • High-Torque Multi-Axis Machining

2D CNC Router Works (Flat & Cut-Through)

Subtractive precision milling across planar rigid substrates—including moisture-resistant HDHMR, solid hardwoods, Corian, and acrylic. Engineered for crisp architectural filigree, high-contrast V-carved typography, and zero-tolerance flush inlays.

Category 02 • Sculptural Relief & Multi-Depth Contours

3D CNC Router Works (Depth, Relief & Contours)

Multi-axis ball-nose and endmill contour sculpting across solid boards and thick hardwood slabs. Delivering multi-depth bas-relief architectural murals, organic parametric wave walls, and continuous fluted textured cladding.

Category 03 • Photopolymer & Additive Synthesis

3D Printing (Additive Manufacturing)

High-fidelity additive manufacturing capturing microscopic surface textures, organic undercuts, and floating sculptural elements. Engineered in tough photopolymer resin and fiber composites for sacred temple icons, lifelike memorial busts, and complex gallery art pieces.
