The Art of Architectural Jali: Precision CNC Lattice Cutting in Modern Interiors

CNC Routing October 02, 2026 5 Min Read
Precision CNC jali lattice routing on calibrated moisture-resistant HDHMR • Guwahati Atelier

In classical Indian architecture, the jali was both a functional climate regulator and a sacred ornament. Today, computer numerical control (CNC) routing has liberated this heritage, allowing architects and homeowners to sculpt intricate light-filtering screens with micrometer precision.

Whether defining a pooja sanctuary, introducing visual seclusion between living and dining suites, or providing an elegant facade for wardrobe doors, CNC-cut jali panels have become an essential element in luxury residential interiors. But behind the serene visual play of light and shadow lies a rigorous manufacturing science.

Material Selection: Beyond Standard MDF

The most common mistake in architectural lattice work is selecting standard commercial MDF. When delicate filigree ribbons are cut into standard fiberboard, ambient moisture causes swelling, warping, and frayed edges that absorb paint unevenly.

At Tridimensional Creation, we engineer lattices strictly on substrate formulations tailored for dimensional stability:

  • High-Density High Moisture Resistant (HDHMR): Compacted at superior pressure with polyurethane bonding, providing razor-sharp edge retention that does not swell in North-East Indian humid conditions.
  • Wood Polymer Composite (WPC): 100% waterproof and termite-proof, ideal for bathroom privacy louvers, exterior balconies, and damp partition walls.
  • Solid Hardwoods (Teak, Steam Beach, White Oak): Machined using multi-pass downward spirals for organic warmth and hand-finished oil finishes.
  • Solid Surface Acrylic (Corian): Seamless, monolithic screens capable of backlit translucent radiance.
“A great jali panel is not merely carved wood; it is a calculated instrument that choreographs light, air, and privacy across interior spaces.”

Tooling Strategies & Feed-Rate Optimization

Intricate geometries like radiating leaf petals or micro-geometric Islamic stars demand precise end-mill selection. Rapid tool changeovers between roughing hoggers and 1.5mm carbide compression bits ensure that sharp inner corners are resolved without burning the substrate.

Our CNC routers operate under multi-zone vacuum clamping tables. By immobilizing the sheet evenly across its entire surface, vibration chatter is eliminated—ensuring every curved petal edge remains buttery smooth prior to primer application.

Finishing Protocols: The Factory PU Advantage

On-site manual brush painting inevitably clogs micro-details and leaves unsightly runs inside deep cutouts. To preserve the crispness of our CNC profiles, panels undergo our three-stage finishing protocol:

  1. High-Build Isolation Primer: Sealed under pneumatic spray to prevent grain soakage.
  2. Intermediate De-Nibbing: Hand-sanded by artisans to eliminate micro-burrs.
  3. Factory Polyurethane (PU) Coat: Applied in matte, satin, or high-gloss formulations with non-yellowing UV stabilizers.
