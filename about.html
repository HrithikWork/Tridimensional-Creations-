The Origin Story

The Foundation (July 2024)

For over seven years, K. Sanjeev Singha (CEO) mastered the craft of high-detail 3D digital sculpting using ZBrush, Maya, and Blender. He spent thousands of hours sculpting complex anatomy, delicate organic flowing forms, and intricate spatial geometries with micro-level precision.

Yet, a frustrating conflict kept happening: beautiful, organic digital sculpts were routinely ruined once sent to standard machine shops. Traditional job shops only understood flat, basic 2D geometries and rigid CAD lines. They stripped away the organic subtlety, shaved off fine curves, and flattened the artistic depth that made the original design extraordinary.

To protect the integrity of the art, we built Tridimensional Creation Pvt Ltd to bridge the gap. We own the machines so we can ensure the final physical piece has the exact soul, depth, and detail of the original digital sculpt. When you work with us, the artists who inspect your references and sculpt your 3D digital models are the very same specialists programming and running the CNC spindles and 3D printers.
